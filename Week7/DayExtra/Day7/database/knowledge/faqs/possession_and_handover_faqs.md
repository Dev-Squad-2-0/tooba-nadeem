# Possession & Handover FAQs

**Q: Is the possession date listed in a project brochure guaranteed?**
A: No. Possession dates represent the developer's current projected timeline and may shift due to construction, regulatory, or supply-related factors, particularly for under-construction projects.

**Q: What documents do I receive at possession?**
A: Typically a possession/handover letter from the developer confirming the unit is ready for occupancy, along with instructions for utility connection transfer and, where applicable, society/owners'-association membership.

**Q: When does title transfer happen relative to possession?**
A: This varies by project type. For some plot-based and villa schemes (e.g. Emerald Gardens), individual title transfer may already be underway or completed at time of listing. For apartment projects still under construction, title transfer processes typically begin after possession, once the developer completes any required subdivision/allotment formalities with the relevant authority.

**Q: What if my project's possession date has passed and I haven't received possession?**
A: Buyers should first raise the matter directly with the developer's customer service or the assigned sales agent for that project, requesting a written updated timeline. Persistent, unresolved delays can also be raised with the relevant development authority (LDA, SBCA, CDA, or RDA) under whose jurisdiction the project falls.

**Q: Are society/maintenance charges due before or after possession?**
A: Maintenance and society charges generally begin from the point of possession/handover, once common-area facilities (see each project's amenities list) become operational and chargeable to owners.
