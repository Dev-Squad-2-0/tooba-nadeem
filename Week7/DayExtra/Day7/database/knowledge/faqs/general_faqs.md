# General FAQs

**Q: Is Meridian Homes Pakistan the developer of these projects?**
A: No. Meridian Homes Pakistan is a marketing and advisory platform that lists projects from partner developers. Each project's brochure names its actual developer, who is responsible for construction, possession, and title.

**Q: How many projects and cities does Meridian Homes Pakistan currently cover?**
A: 8 active projects across 4 partner developers in 4 cities: Islamabad, Karachi, Lahore, Rawalpindi.

**Q: Are the prices listed final?**
A: Prices reflect the developer's current price list at the time of inquiry and are subject to change until a booking amount is paid and a booking confirmation is issued.

**Q: Can I speak to a human agent instead of the voice agent?**
A: Yes. Every project has an assigned sales agent listed in its brochure, and general inquiries can be directed to the relevant city desk or head office contact listed in the company contact information document.

**Q: Does Meridian Homes Pakistan charge buyers a fee to inquire or book?**
A: No. Meridian Homes Pakistan's services to buyers are free; the platform is compensated by partner developers for qualified referrals, consistent with standard real estate marketplace practice.

**Q: What if I want to compare projects across cities?**
A: The voice agent and recommendation engine can compare available projects by city, price range, unit type, and possession status based on your stated preferences (e.g. budget, city, ready vs. under-construction).
