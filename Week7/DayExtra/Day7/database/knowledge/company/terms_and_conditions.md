## 1. Role of Meridian Homes Pakistan
Meridian Homes Pakistan acts as a marketing and advisory intermediary between buyers and partner developers. Meridian Homes Pakistan is not the developer, builder, or title-holder of any listed project. All construction, possession, and title-transfer obligations remain with the respective developer named in each project's brochure and developer profile.

## 2. Booking Process
A booking is only confirmed once (a) the buyer has paid the applicable booking amount as specified in the project's payment plan, and (b) a booking confirmation has been issued in the buyer's name by the developer's sales office. Verbal or voice-agent-provided quotations are indicative and subject to written confirmation.

## 3. Price Validity
Prices quoted through Meridian Homes Pakistan's voice agent, website, or sales staff reflect the developer's price list at the time of inquiry and may change without notice until a booking is confirmed and the booking amount is paid.

## 4. Cancellation & Refunds
Cancellation and refund terms are set by the individual developer and vary by project and payment plan. Buyers should confirm the specific cancellation policy for their chosen project with the assigned sales agent before booking. As a general industry practice in Pakistan, booking amounts are frequently non-refundable or partially refundable after a processing deduction; this must be confirmed in writing at time of booking.

## 5. No Guarantee of Possession Date
Possession dates listed in project brochures are the developer's current projected timelines and are not guaranteed. Construction-linked projects may experience delays due to regulatory, material supply, or weather-related factors.

These terms are governed by the laws of the Islamic Republic of Pakistan. Any disputes are subject to the jurisdiction of the courts of the city in which the relevant project is located.
