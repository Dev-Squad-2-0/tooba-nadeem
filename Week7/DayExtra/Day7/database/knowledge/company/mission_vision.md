# Mission, Vision & Values — Meridian Homes Pakistan

## Mission
To make property buying, selling and renting in Pakistan transparent, verifiable, and accessible — whether the buyer is in Lahore or overseas.

## Vision
To become the most trusted digital front door to Pakistan's real estate market, where every listing is verified against developer records and every price is traceable to a real payment plan.

## Values

1. **Verified Listings Only** — Every project listed through Meridian Homes Pakistan is cross-checked against the developer's registration with the relevant development authority (LDA, SBCA, CDA, or RDA) before publication.
2. **No Hidden Charges** — All prices quoted include the base unit price; applicable transfer, registration, and membership charges are disclosed separately and explicitly, never bundled silently into the quoted price.
3. **Data Consistency** — Our structured database and marketing brochures are kept in sync; the price, payment plan, and possession date a buyer hears from an agent will match what is filed with the developer.
4. **Respectful, No-Pressure Sales** — Agents are trained to inform, not pressure. Site visits and follow-ups are scheduled at the buyer's convenience.
