## About

Capital Heights Group is an Islamabad-based developer founded in 2010, focused on planned residential enclaves and mid-rise apartment blocks near the Islamabad Expressway and I-8/I-10 sectors. The group is registered with the Capital Development Authority (CDA) and is known for plot-and-house hybrid schemes aimed at overseas Pakistani investors.
