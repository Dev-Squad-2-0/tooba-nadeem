## Overview

Horizon Business Bay is a mixed-use tower on Shahrah-e-Faisal combining compact 1 and 2-bedroom rental-focused apartments on the upper floors with corporate office suites on floors 3 through 9, aimed at investors seeking rental yield near Karachi's commercial corridor.

Horizon Business Bay is developed by Metro Horizon Builders, a Karachi-headquartered developer founded in 2005 with 9 completed projects to date. For more on the developer's track record, see the Metro Horizon Builders company profile in the developers/ folder.
