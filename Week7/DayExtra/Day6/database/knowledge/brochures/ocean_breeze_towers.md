## Overview

Ocean Breeze Towers is a twin-tower, 28-storey residential development in Clifton Block 5, roughly 900 meters from Sea View, offering sea-facing 1 to 4-bedroom apartments and a duplex Sky Villa collection on the top four floors. The development includes a dedicated retail podium on the ground and first floors.

Ocean Breeze Towers is developed by Metro Horizon Builders, a Karachi-headquartered developer founded in 2005 with 9 completed projects to date. For more on the developer's track record, see the Metro Horizon Builders company profile in the developers/ folder.
