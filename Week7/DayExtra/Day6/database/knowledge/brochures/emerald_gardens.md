## Overview

Emerald Gardens is a low-rise gated villa community on Raiwind Road offering 5-marla, 10-marla and 1-kanal double-storey homes with private lawns, built around a 12-acre central park. All units are fully constructed and available for immediate possession with individual title transfer already underway.

Emerald Gardens is developed by Silk Developers (Pvt) Ltd, a Lahore-headquartered developer founded in 1998 with 14 completed projects to date. For more on the developer's track record, see the Silk Developers (Pvt) Ltd company profile in the developers/ folder.
