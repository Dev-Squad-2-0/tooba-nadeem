# Property Verification & Legal Guide

## Why Verification Matters
Pakistan's real estate market includes both fully authorized developments and unauthorized or informally marketed schemes. Verifying a project before booking protects buyers from delayed possession, inability to transfer title, or outright fraud.

## What to Verify

### 1. Developer Registration
Confirm the developer's registration number with the relevant authority:
- **Lahore Development Authority (LDA)** for Lahore-based projects
- **Sindh Building Control Authority (SBCA)** for Karachi-based projects
- **Capital Development Authority (CDA)** for Islamabad-based projects
- **Rawalpindi Development Authority (RDA)** for Rawalpindi-based projects

Every project brochure in this system lists a registration number under "Registration" — buyers should independently confirm this number is current and valid with the issuing authority before booking, as registrations can lapse or be revoked.

### 2. Approved Layout Plan (NOC)
Ask the developer for the No-Objection Certificate (NOC) and approved layout plan for the specific phase/block you are purchasing into — a scheme-wide NOC does not automatically cover every later phase.

### 3. Land Title Chain
For plot-based schemes especially, request evidence of the developer's clean title to the underlying land, including any mutation (intiqal) records, before the scheme was launched.

### 4. Payment Trail
Always pay through crossed cheque, pay order, or bank transfer directly to the developer's official company account — never to an individual agent's personal account — and retain all receipts.

## Red Flags
- Verbal-only assurances about approvals with no willingness to share documentation.
- Heavy pressure to pay in cash to avoid "documentation delays."
- Prices significantly below comparable projects in the same area with no clear explanation.
- Reluctance to provide a written, signed booking confirmation after payment.

## Where to Get Help
Buyers with concerns about a specific project's registration status can contact the relevant development authority's public information desk directly, in addition to requesting documentation from the developer.
