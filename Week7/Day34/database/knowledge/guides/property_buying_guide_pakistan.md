# Property Buying Guide in Pakistan

## Step 1: Define Your Budget and Financing Route
Decide whether you are purchasing via full cash payment, a developer installment plan, or bank mortgage financing. Most under-construction projects in this system (e.g. Skyline Residency, Ocean Breeze Towers, Capital Greens Enclave, Al-Noor Valley) offer multi-year installment plans; ready projects (e.g. Emerald Gardens, The Pearl Heights, Al-Noor Business Square) are typically purchased via cash or bank financing since no developer installment plan is offered once possession is ready.

## Step 2: Verify the Project and Developer
Before booking, confirm:
- The developer's registration number with the relevant authority (LDA in Lahore, SBCA in Karachi, CDA in Islamabad, or RDA in Rawalpindi/twin cities).
- The project's own registration/approval number, listed at the top of every project brochure in this system.
- That the specific block/phase you are buying into has approved layout plans, not just the overall scheme.

## Step 3: Choose Your Unit and Payment Plan
Compare unit types, sizes, and price ranges across projects that match your budget and city preference. Review the specific payment plan(s) attached to that project — booking percentage, down payment, installment frequency, and possession payment — before proceeding.

## Step 4: Book the Unit
Booking typically requires:
1. A completed booking form with CNIC/passport copy.
2. Payment of the booking amount specified in the payment plan.
3. Issuance of a signed booking confirmation/allotment letter by the developer.

## Step 5: Track Installments and Construction Progress
For installment-based purchases, keep records of each payment against the schedule in your payment plan. For construction-linked plans, some installments may be tied to construction milestones rather than fixed dates — confirm which model applies to your project.

## Step 6: Possession and Handover
At possession, the remaining balance (typically the final possession-payment percentage in your plan) is due. The developer issues a possession letter, and utility connections and society dues transfer to the owner from that point forward, subject to the specific project's handover process.
