# Payment & Installment FAQs

**Q: What is the difference between "booking amount" and "down payment"?**
A: The booking amount is paid at the time of initial reservation to hold the unit. The down payment is an additional payment, typically due shortly after booking (commonly within 30 days), before the installment schedule begins. Both are specified as percentages in each project's payment plan.

**Q: Are installment plans interest-free?**
A: Standard developer installment plans listed in this system do not carry a mark-up/interest charge for on-time payments; however, late payments may attract a surcharge per the individual developer's policy. This is distinct from bank mortgage financing, which does carry a mark-up or profit rate.

**Q: What happens if I miss an installment?**
A: Policies vary by developer and are not standardized across projects in this system. Typically, a grace period applies before a late-payment surcharge is triggered, and prolonged non-payment can result in cancellation per the developer's terms. Buyers should confirm the specific late-payment policy for their project with the assigned sales agent at time of booking.

**Q: Why do some projects (e.g. Emerald Gardens, The Pearl Heights, Al-Noor Business Square) not offer an installment plan?**
A: These are fully constructed, ready-for-possession projects. Once a project has reached ready status, developers in this system typically require full cash payment or third-party bank financing rather than offering a multi-year developer installment plan.

**Q: Can I switch from one payment plan to another after booking?**
A: This depends on the specific developer's policy and is not guaranteed. Buyers wishing to switch plans (for example, from a longer installment plan to a fast-track plan) should raise this with their assigned sales agent before making further payments.

**Q: Is the possession payment the same as final possession costs?**
A: The "possession payment" percentage in a payment plan refers to the remaining balance of the unit price due at handover. It is separate from possession-stage charges such as membership fees or utility connection charges, which are typically communicated separately by the developer closer to handover.
