## Overview

Capital Greens Enclave is a planned residential scheme off the Islamabad Expressway offering both undeveloped plots and turnkey built houses in 8-marla to 1-kanal sizes. Roads, sewerage and electrification for Phase 1 are complete; Phase 2 development works are ongoing.

Capital Greens Enclave is developed by Capital Heights Group, a Islamabad-headquartered developer founded in 2010 with 6 completed projects to date. For more on the developer's track record, see the Capital Heights Group company profile in the developers/ folder.
