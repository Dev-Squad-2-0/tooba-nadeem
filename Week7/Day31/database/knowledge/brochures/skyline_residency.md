## Overview

Skyline Residency is a 22-storey gated apartment tower rising above DHA Phase 6, offering 2, 3 and 4-bedroom residences with reinforced-glass facades and dedicated servant quarters in the larger units. The project is built around a central landscaped courtyard and is positioned as Silk Developers' flagship high-rise in Lahore.

Skyline Residency is developed by Silk Developers (Pvt) Ltd, a Lahore-headquartered developer founded in 1998 with 14 completed projects to date. For more on the developer's track record, see the Silk Developers (Pvt) Ltd company profile in the developers/ folder.
