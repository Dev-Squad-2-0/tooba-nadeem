## Overview

The Pearl Heights is a 12-storey mid-rise apartment block near I-8 Markaz offering 2 and 3-bedroom units, fully constructed and handed over. It is Capital Heights Group's first completed vertical project and is fully occupied with active owners' association management.

The Pearl Heights is developed by Capital Heights Group, a Islamabad-headquartered developer founded in 2010 with 6 completed projects to date. For more on the developer's track record, see the Capital Heights Group company profile in the developers/ folder.
