# Privacy Policy — Meridian Homes Pakistan

## Information We Collect
When you interact with our voice agent, website, or sales staff, we may collect: your name, phone number, email address, CNIC/passport number (for booking purposes only), city of residence, and project preferences.

## How We Use Your Information
- To respond to inquiries and schedule site visits.
- To share your contact details with the relevant partner developer once you express firm booking interest.
- To send payment reminders if you have an active booking with installment plans.
- To improve our recommendation engine's understanding of buyer preferences (aggregated and anonymized where possible).

## Information Sharing
We share buyer information with the specific developer of the project the buyer has expressed interest in, solely for the purpose of processing that inquiry or booking. We do not sell buyer data to third parties or unrelated developers.

## Data Retention
Inquiry data is retained for 24 months from the last interaction unless a booking is made, in which case records are retained for the duration of the payment plan plus 7 years, consistent with standard financial record-keeping practice in Pakistan.

## Your Rights
You may request a copy of the information we hold about you, or request deletion of inquiry data (where no active booking exists), by emailing hello@meridianhomes.pk.
