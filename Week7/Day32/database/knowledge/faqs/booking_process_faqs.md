# Booking Process FAQs

**Q: How do I book a unit through Meridian Homes Pakistan?**
A: Identify a project and unit type of interest (via the voice agent, recommendation engine, or by speaking to the assigned sales agent), confirm the applicable payment plan, and submit the booking amount along with a completed booking form and CNIC/passport copy to the developer's sales office.

**Q: Can I book a site visit before deciding?**
A: Yes. Site visits — in-person or virtual — can be scheduled directly with the assigned sales agent for any project, synced to the agent's calendar availability.

**Q: Do I need to pay anything to schedule a site visit or get more information?**
A: No. Site visits, brochures, and general project information are provided free of charge; payment is only required once you choose to proceed with an actual unit booking.

**Q: Can I reserve a unit temporarily without paying the full booking amount?**
A: This depends on the individual developer's policy and is not standardized across all projects in this system. Some developers may offer a short, non-binding hold pending payment; buyers should confirm this directly with the assigned sales agent for their chosen project.

**Q: What happens after I pay the booking amount?**
A: The developer issues a signed booking confirmation/allotment letter, and you are entered into the payment plan schedule you selected, with the down payment (where applicable) typically due within the following 30 days.

**Q: Can I transfer my booking to someone else later?**
A: Many developers allow booking/allotment transfers subject to a transfer fee and their internal approval process, but this is not guaranteed across every project. Confirm the specific transfer policy for your project with the assigned sales agent before booking if this flexibility is important to you.
