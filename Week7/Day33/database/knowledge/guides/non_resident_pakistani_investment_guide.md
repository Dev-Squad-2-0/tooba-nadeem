# Overseas Pakistani (Non-Resident) Investment Guide

## Can Overseas Pakistanis Buy Property in Pakistan?
Yes. Overseas Pakistanis (including dual nationals) can purchase residential and commercial property in Pakistan on the same basis as resident Pakistanis, and several projects in this system — particularly plot and installment-based schemes such as Capital Greens Enclave and Al-Noor Valley — are actively marketed to overseas buyers.

## Documentation Typically Required
- Valid CNIC or NICOP (National Identity Card for Overseas Pakistanis)
- Passport copy
- Proof of overseas address (utility bill, bank statement, or residency permit)
- Power of Attorney (if a representative in Pakistan will handle site visits, payments, or possession on the buyer's behalf)

## Making Payments from Abroad
Payments should be routed through proper banking channels — either a Foreign Currency Value Account, Non-Resident Rupee Account, or Roshan Digital Account (RDA) — directly to the developer's declared company account, with transaction records retained for tax and audit purposes.

## Roshan Digital Account (RDA)
The State Bank of Pakistan's Roshan Digital Account initiative allows Non-Resident Pakistanis to open an account remotely and invest in real estate (including via Naya Pakistan Certificates and direct property purchases) from abroad. Buyers using RDA to fund a purchase should confirm the receiving developer's account is set up to accept RDA-based transfers.

## Power of Attorney (POA) for Remote Buyers
Overseas buyers who cannot visit in person for booking, site visits, or possession typically execute a Power of Attorney (attested at the relevant Pakistani consulate/embassy) authorizing a trusted representative — a family member or the assigned sales agent — to complete these steps on their behalf. The specific project's sales agent (listed in that project's brochure) can guide on the exact POA format their developer requires.

## Site Visits Without Travel
For overseas buyers who prefer not to travel before booking, virtual site visits (video walkthroughs with the assigned agent) can be scheduled in place of an in-person visit; this is available for both under-construction and ready projects listed in this system.
