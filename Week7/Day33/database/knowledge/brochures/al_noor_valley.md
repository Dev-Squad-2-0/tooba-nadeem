## Overview

Al-Noor Valley is a large residential plot scheme on Adiala Road aimed at end-users and first-time buyers in the Rawalpindi market, with phased possession as development milestones (roads, water supply, boundary wall) are completed in each block.

Al-Noor Valley is developed by Al-Noor Estates & Developers, a Rawalpindi-headquartered developer founded in 2001 with 11 completed projects to date. For more on the developer's track record, see the Al-Noor Estates & Developers company profile in the developers/ folder.
