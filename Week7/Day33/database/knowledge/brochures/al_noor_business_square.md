## Overview

Al-Noor Business Square is a completed 6-storey commercial plaza on Murree Road with ground and mezzanine retail shops, third and fourth-floor offices, and a rooftop food court, fully handed over with an active tenant mix of retail and F&B brands.

Al-Noor Business Square is developed by Al-Noor Estates & Developers, a Rawalpindi-headquartered developer founded in 2001 with 11 completed projects to date. For more on the developer's track record, see the Al-Noor Estates & Developers company profile in the developers/ folder.
