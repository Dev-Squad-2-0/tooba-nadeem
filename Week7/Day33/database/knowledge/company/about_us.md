# About Meridian Homes Pakistan

## Who We Are

Meridian Homes Pakistan is a real estate marketplace and advisory platform operating across Islamabad, Karachi, Lahore, Rawalpindi. We do not develop projects ourselves; instead, we partner with vetted developers — including Silk Developers (Pvt) Ltd, Metro Horizon Builders, Capital Heights Group, Al-Noor Estates & Developers — to list, market and manage inquiries for their residential and commercial projects on behalf of buyers, investors, and overseas Pakistanis.

## What We Do

- **Property Discovery:** We maintain a structured, continuously updated database of active projects, unit availability, and price ranges across our partner developers.
- **Buyer Advisory:** Our sales agents are assigned by project and city, and are trained on the specific payment plans, unit inventory, and possession timelines of the projects they represent.
- **Site Visit Scheduling:** Buyers can book in-person or virtual site visits directly through our voice agent or web portal, synced to our agents' calendars.
- **Post-Booking Support:** We assist with payment plan tracking, installment reminders, and possession-stage document coordination in partnership with the respective developer.

## Our Coverage
