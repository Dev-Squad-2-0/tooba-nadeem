## About

Silk Developers (Pvt) Ltd is a Lahore-based real estate development house founded in 1998, known for large-scale gated residential communities along DHA and Raiwind Road. The company is registered with the Lahore Development Authority (LDA) and has delivered over 14 completed housing and apartment projects across Lahore, with a reputation for on-time possession and in-house facilities management post-handover.
