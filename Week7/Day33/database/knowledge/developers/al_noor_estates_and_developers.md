## About

Al-Noor Estates & Developers is a Rawalpindi-based real estate firm operating since 2001, with a portfolio spanning residential plot schemes along Adiala Road and commercial plazas on Murree Road. The company is registered with the Rawalpindi Development Authority (RDA) and has built a client base largely through referral-driven sales in twin-city (Rawalpindi-Islamabad) markets.
