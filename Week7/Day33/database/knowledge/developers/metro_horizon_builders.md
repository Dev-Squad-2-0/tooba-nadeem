## About

Metro Horizon Builders is a Karachi-headquartered developer established in 2005, specializing in high-rise residential towers and mixed-use commercial developments along Clifton and Shahrah-e-Faisal. The company is registered with the Sindh Building Control Authority (SBCA) and is recognized for waterfront-adjacent apartment towers with in-house retail podiums.
