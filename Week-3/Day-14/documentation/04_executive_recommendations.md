## Task 6 — Executive Recommendations

### Business Opportunities
1. **Target high-value customers** — `customer_segments` shows Platinum/Gold customers driving a disproportionate share of revenue. Focused loyalty offers can increase their lifetime value.
2. **Expand strong territories** — `territory_metrics` identifies top-revenue regions (see Visualization 3). Additional sales investment here has the highest ROI potential.
3. **Promote top-margin products** — `product_profitability` and Visualization 6 highlight the products generating the most revenue; marketing spend should prioritize these.
4. **Grow weak categories** — `category_performance` (Visualization 5) shows underperforming categories that could benefit from repricing or bundling.
5. **Prevent stockouts on fast movers** — `low_stock_products` combined with Visualization 8 shows which high-demand items need urgent restocking.

### Business Risks
1. **Inventory shortages** — Visualization 8 shows a meaningful share of products in "Low Stock," risking missed sales.
2. **Revenue volatility** — Visualization 2 (`sales_growth`) shows months with negative growth, signaling possible demand or operational issues.
3. **Customer concentration** — `customer_lifetime_value` shows revenue reliance on a small number of high-spending customers, creating vulnerability if they churn.
4. **Underperforming territories** — `lowest_performing_territories` shows regions lagging significantly behind the average (Visualization 3), dragging down overall growth.
5. **Supplier reliability** — `supplier_performance` may show vendors with long average lead times, risking stockouts and delayed replenishment.

### Actionable Recommendations
1. Launch targeted retention campaigns for Platinum/Gold segments identified in `customer_segments`.
2. Prioritize replenishment for products flagged "Low Stock" in `inventory_metrics`.
3. Allocate additional sales/marketing resources to the bottom 5 territories in `lowest_performing_territories`.
4. Introduce a recognition/incentive program for top-ranked employees in `employee_rankings`.
5. Renegotiate terms or diversify suppliers with the longest lead times per `supplier_performance`.
